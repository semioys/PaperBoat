hd_standard.bin - HD dialogue font for PaperBoat

The glyphs in hd_standard.bin are rendered from Montserrat
(Montserrat-Regular.ttf in this folder) by tools/switch/gen_hd_font.ps1,
emboldened and fitted to the game's character widths. No glyph art from the
original game is used.
